How it works

Three steps. You stay in charge of the deal from first email to sign-off.

Tell Gilet your numbers

Your ideal fee, your walk-away figure and anything not to push on. Two minutes, once per deal.

Copy Gilet in

When the client talks budget, add Gilet to the email thread. It drafts a reply that anchors on the value of your work.

Approve and win

You approve each message. Gilet handles the back-and-forth on rate, scope, deposits and payment terms until you have a deal you’re happy with.

Built to protect the relationship

Clients hire people they like working with. Gilet negotiates the way a good agent would: politely, clearly and with options.

Questions

Will my client know an AI is involved?

Yes. Every message Gilet drafts is signed as your AI assistant and says that you approve each one. Being upfront is part of how Gilet keeps trust with your client.

Can Gilet agree a deal without me?

No. Gilet never sends anything you haven’t approved, never goes below your walk-away figure and can’t sign or commit to terms on your behalf. The final yes is always yours.

What if my client wants to talk to me instead?

Gilet steps back straight away and the conversation carries on between you and your client.

What happens to my data? (UK GDPR)

Harmony Living Ltd handles your data under UK GDPR. Gilet only reads the email threads you copy it into, and uses them only to negotiate your deal. We don’t sell your data. You can ask us to see or delete it at any time by emailing jobbot+gilet@mail.grokbot.com.

Is this legal advice?

No. Gilet helps with commercial terms such as rate, scope and payment schedules. It isn’t a law firm and doesn’t give legal advice. If a contract or legal clause comes up, it flags it to you so you can get proper advice.
